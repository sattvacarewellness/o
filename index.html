What SattvaCare Wellness Does

SattvaCare Wellness helps individuals break destructive lifestyle patterns, restore health and clarity, and extend healthy human lifespan using Indian Knowledge Systems validated by science.

Our Core System

We operate through a structured framework called Purified Performance Prolonging — Purification, Performance, and Prolonging.

Purification

De-addiction through systematic Yoga, detoxification based on Vedic Naturopathy, and inner stability through Bhakti of God (Krishna), guided by Ashtanga Yoga.

Performance

Enhancing health, discipline, values, and wisdom using Ayurveda, Bhagavad Gītā, and Nīti Śāstras for real-world excellence.

Prolonging

Slowing biological ageing and enhancing vitality using Yoga, Ayurveda, and Rasa-Śāstra. Classical Indian frameworks describe the potential of 150+ year lifespans under ideal conditions. SattvaCare Wellness works toward this theoretical upper potential by prioritizing youthfulness and vitality.
